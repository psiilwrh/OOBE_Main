FAQ

Questions we hear first.

What does Out of Box Experience Inc. (OOBE) do?
OOBE is a Canadian IT, cloud and unified communications company based in Toronto. We provide consulting, implementation and ongoing support for IT systems, cloud, Microsoft 365 and Azure, AI deployment and governance, virtualization, backup and disaster recovery, cyber awareness training, and Cloud IP Phone business phone systems.
Who does OOBE work with?
Businesses and organizations that want secure, scalable and reliable technology, from small offices to larger teams, whether they need a one-time project or a long-term IT partner.
Which areas do you serve?
We are based in Toronto and work with clients across the Greater Toronto Area, Canada and the United States, delivered remotely or on site.
Can OOBE help our business adopt AI?
Yes. We assess readiness, identify the best use cases, deploy tools such as Microsoft Copilot securely, and put written policy and governance in place so AI is used safely and responsibly.
Can you move us to the cloud or Microsoft 365?
Yes. We plan and carry out migrations to Microsoft 365 and Azure, including Exchange Online and hybrid deployments, and design multi-cloud and hybrid architectures.
