About us

Who we are

Your partner for IT, cloud and communications.

Out of Box Experience Inc. (OOBE) provides IT, cloud and unified communications services to modern businesses. We combine strategic insight with hands-on delivery, so the same team that plans your solution builds and supports it.

Our work spans IT and cloud consulting, Microsoft solutions, AI deployment and governance, virtualization, backup and disaster recovery, cyber awareness training and Cloud IP Phone systems.

We are a Canadian company based in Toronto, working with clients across Canada and the United States, from local businesses in the GTA to nationwide projects.
