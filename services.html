FAQ

Service questions.

Do you offer AI consulting, implementation and governance?
Yes. We provide AI strategy (readiness assessments, use cases and roadmaps), implement AI securely, including Microsoft Copilot, agents and automations integrated with your systems, write AI policies, and set up governance with risk controls, monitoring and compliance.
Can you write an AI policy for our company?
Yes. We define acceptable use, data handling and privacy rules, approved tools and Responsible AI principles, then help you roll them out and keep them current.
Which virtualization platforms do you support?
We design, implement and migrate VMware and Microsoft Hyper-V environments, and provide performance optimization and proactive monitoring.
Can you back up Microsoft 365?
Yes. Our backup and disaster recovery services include Microsoft 365 data protection, on-prem and cloud backup design, recovery planning and regular drill testing.
Do you provide cybersecurity awareness training?
Yes. Training covers phishing and social engineering, safe email and social media use, everyday security habits and building a security-aware culture.
Do you support multi-cloud and hybrid environments?
Yes. We design and support IaaS, PaaS and SaaS across multi-cloud and hybrid architectures, and help govern cost and security.
What is unified communications (UCaaS)?
It brings business calling, messaging, meetings and contact centre onto one cloud platform. We deliver it through Cloud IP Phone, including Teams calling, cloud PBX, SMS and fax, and AI contact centre.
One-time projects or ongoing support?
Both. We deliver individual projects such as migrations and deployments, and provide long-term support, monitoring and documentation.
