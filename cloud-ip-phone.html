FAQ

Cloud IP Phone questions.

What is Cloud IP Phone?
OOBE's unified communications (UCaaS) platform: a cloud-hosted VoIP business phone system that replaces an on-site PBX and works on IP desk phones, computers, mobile devices and Microsoft Teams.
Can we keep our existing numbers?
Yes. We port your business numbers and run old and new systems in parallel during cutover, so calls are not interrupted.
Does it work with Microsoft Teams?
Yes. With Teams Direct Routing, Teams becomes your phone system. You can also send and receive SMS, MMS, WhatsApp and faxes inside Teams.
Where is it hosted?
In Canada, on carrier-grade infrastructure with active-active failover across regions, monitored 24/7.
Which phones can we use?
IP desk phones from Yealink, Polycom, Fanvil, Grandstream, Snom and Cisco, delivered pre-configured, or a softphone on browser, desktop or mobile.
Is 911 supported?
Yes. NG911 emergency calling is included with phone numbers.
How is it priced?
Cloud PBX is per user on monthly or annual terms, with Bronze, Silver and Gold AI plans. SIP trunking, numbers, contact centre and analytics are priced on usage. Contact us for a quote.
